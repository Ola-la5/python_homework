Which sections of the website are restricted for crawling?
Wikipedia blocks crawlers from sensitive and administrative pages, like deletion discussions, spam blacklists, abuse reports, copyright investigations, and arbitration requests.
User account pages (username changes, protected titles, adminship/bureaucratship requests) are also off-limits.
Noticeboards, sandboxes, internal API pages (/w/, /api/), trap pages, and /wiki/Special: pages are restricted too.
Regular encyclopedia articles are allowed.

Are there specific rules for certain user agents?
Yes. Some bots, like MJ12bot and Mediapartners-Google*, are completely blocked. 
Many site-copying or fast/misbehaving bots are also disallowed. 
All other bots (*) can’t access internal wiki pages, API pages, trap pages, or special pages.

Reflect on why websites use robots.txt
The purpose of a robots.txt file is to guide web crawlers on which parts of a website they can access, helping prevent server overload.
It also protects sensitive or private content, promoting ethical scraping and reducing the risk of legal or privacy issues.
